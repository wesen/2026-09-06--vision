Shared evidence and timing

A frame's presentation timestamp (PTS) is its time in the video. Frame number alone is insufficient for variable-rate video. For a requested sample rate f, the decoder selects the first frame at or after each point start + k / f, excludes frames at the end, and removes duplicates. The thumbnails show actual decoded inputs; browser seeking is only a viewing convenience.

All ranges are half-open: [start, end). A crop is stored as exact integer pixels as well as the normalized selection. Every source and selected PNG has a SHA-256 identity. When you change a control, you create a new experiment; an earlier result retains its own settings.

Offline component runs can use the whole selected range. Causal replay additionally restricts evidence by availability time and enforces worker queues. A late answer must not appear in an earlier as-of view. Open the replay viewer to study that distinction.
